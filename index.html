    Traffic Rider

    Traffic Rider is a first-person motorcycle racing game where you speed through busy highways, weaving between vehicles and chasing high scores. Developed by SKgames, it focuses on smooth controls, realistic visuals, and fast-paced gameplay.You ride from a first-person perspective, placing you directly on the bike for a more immersive experience. The objective is simple—go as far and as fast as possible while avoiding crashes and overtaking traffic to earn points.

    What Is Traffic Rider?

    Traffic Rider expands on the endless racing formula by adding more depth and realism. It combines high-speed riding with missions, varied environments, and detailed sound design.With responsive controls, lifelike engine sounds, and dynamic gameplay, it delivers a more engaging experience than typical arcade racing games, while still staying easy to pick up and play.

    Gameplay

    The game features both an endless mode and a structured career mode with missions. You ride through different environments, complete objectives, and unlock new challenges as difficulty increases.Speed and precision are key. The faster you ride and the closer you pass vehicles, the higher your score. Mistakes, however, can end your run instantly.

    How to Play Traffic Rider

    Getting started is straightforward, but improving takes control and timing.

    • Choose Your Bike
      Start with the basic motorcycle and unlock more as you earn cash. Each bike has different stats like speed, handling, and braking.
    • Select a Game Mode
      Pick from different modes, each with its own objectives and challenges.
    • Hit the Road
      Ride through traffic and avoid crashes. The goal is to travel as far as possible while maintaining high speed to earn more points and cash.
    • Earn and Upgrade
      The farther you go and the closer you pass vehicles, the more rewards you get. Use your earnings to upgrade your bike’s performance.
    • Key Focus
      Avoid collisions, manage your speed, and upgrade wisely—those are the only things that actually matter if you want to progress.
    • Controls
      Arrow Keys / Tilt Device – Steer
      Accelerate / Brake – Control speed

    Game Modes

    Traffic Rider includes several modes, each offering a different way to play. You can also choose environments like highway, desert, city, or winter, along with time settings such as noon, evening, or night, and road types like one-way or two-way.

    • Career Mode
      Complete over 90 missions with increasing difficulty and specific objectives.
    • Endless Mode
      Ride without limits and try to achieve the highest distance and score.
    • Time Trial Mode
      Race against the clock and reach checkpoints before time runs out.
    • Free Ride Mode
      Drive freely with no missions or pressure—just focus on riding.

    Controls

    On Mobile:

    • Tilt Device – Steer
    • Hold Screen – Accelerate

    On PC:

    • Left / Right Arrow – Steer
    • Up Arrow – Accelerate

    Features

    • First-Person Perspective
       Experience the road from the rider’s point of view for a more realistic feel
    • Career Mode
       Complete missions and progress through different levels
    • Multiple Motorbikes
       Choose from over 30 bikes, each with unique stats
    • Realistic Sounds
       Engine audio is recorded from real motorcycles for authenticity
    • Detailed Environments
       Ride through highways, city roads, and varied settings

    Why It Stands Out

    Traffic Rider combines arcade-style racing with realistic elements like sound design and perspective. It’s not just about speed you need control, timing, and awareness to survive longer and score higher.
